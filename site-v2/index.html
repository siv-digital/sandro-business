An illustrative case

“Why does the business next door borrow cheaper than we do?”

Lending access

Because banks and borrowers mostly meet by geography. The lender that knows your industry and wants your profile may be two states away, and the one local quote you hold has no competition. Access is the first problem; price is the second. The alliance’s banking relationships span markets, so your loan is priced by lenders competing for it, not by proximity. The shortlist is a senior partner’s call, made for your situation.

The company A $14M distribution businessOne banking relationship, in state
The need A $6.5M facilityExpansion capital plus a refinance
The local quote Prime + 1.75%Personal guarantee, uncapped
The matched quotes Three term sheets, best at Prime + 1.25%Guarantee steps off at 18 months
The P&L effect $32,500 a year50 basis points on $6.5M, before tax

For illustration purposes only. The numbers show the mechanism, not a promised outcome.
