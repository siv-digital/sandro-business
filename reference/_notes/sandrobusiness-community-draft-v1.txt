Business Owner Community Platform (DRAFT)
Positioning, Provider Network, Content & Process

The concept work so far, positioning, channels, monetization, EPI, and a website
mockup brief, is strong tactical thinking, but a few pieces need to be built out
concretely. Everything here is meant to be marked up, not accepted as-is.

1. POSITIONING: WHAT DIFFERENTIATES US?

  - Draft tagline: "A private collective for business owners preparing to grow or
    sell, with direct access to Sandro's exit-readiness process and a vetted
    network of M&A, valuation, attorneys, growth partners." Need alternatives to
    "collective"
  - Source of actionable, relevant and timely Intelligence: Get educated,
    building relationships, gaining trust in the eco-system partners
  - Eco-system: provide intelligence. Relevant + also have an expert available
  - Incoming requests: Get connected with eco-system partners
  - Outgoing topics: Cases + Newsletter + Client success transition announcement
  - Put broad content with deeper engagement available if owner is interested
  - Calendly links with eco-system partners
  - L/S, Asset protection, CRAT don't care about SIC/NAICS
  - A dedicated Alts-goes-mainstream-like anchor podcast that interviews
    SIC-specific business owners
  - Marketing: Cross connections / promotions with Trade Associations, advertise
    in their newsletters. Get the Glass manufacturers Seal of Approval
  - Reach out to the trade associations and co-market with the major
    associations, cross podcast, cross-advertise
  - Access to actionable SMEs: Value Exchange via 20 min calls
  - Community: Interact with others in events etc.
  - Events, Forums, Webinars, local chapters
  - A platform to help them exit: Exit at your terms

One-line internal framing: This is a client-acquisition funnel with a brand halo,
not a standalone business (??). It exists to produce qualified introductions into
Sandro's core advisory process (Maybe not). Maybe we do not call this platform
Sandro collective, but call the investment management piece Sandro. Member count
and content output are not the metrics that matter, but rather introductions
made, advisory engagements opened, and AUM won.

What this rules out: No positioning language about "networking," "peer
community," or "content hub" as the lead, since that's EPI and Kitces' territory
and competing there head-on is a losing frame. The site, directory, and content
all exist to support the access-to-Sandro claim above, not to stand alone as a
media property.

Branding: Sandro Business Owner Advisory Exchange/Collective OR Sandro Business
Owner Consortium

2. SCOPE: SECTIONS OF THE INITIAL MOCK-UP BUILD WITH FURTHER ENHANCEMENTS

Recommend five sections for the mockup, no more. Each earns its place; a fifth
(events, member portal, etc.) can wait for a real build.

  - Home: the page that has to convert a skeptical owner in ten seconds. Get the
    benefits mentioned above. One time free. 5 things that members benefit
  - Transactional interchange, incoming requests: Get connected with eco-system
    partners, 20 min call with SME
  - Business Consulting problem: You call me with your business problem, I will
    give you the eco-system partner that will help you solve the problem
  - Membership & Apply: the interview gate shown as a real flow, not just a
    contact form
  - Provider Directory (sample): 3 to 4 categories, 2 to 3 firms each, GFOC-style
    category pages
  - Insights (lean): a handful of flagship pieces, not a full content hub, see
    Section 5

3. AUDIENCE: WHO IS THE TARGET?

The site should read as owner-facing, not dual-audience. The moment advisors
appear as a co-equal audience on the homepage, owners read it as a referral
network instead of an exclusive community, which breaks the positioning.

Advisors get a separate, secondary path, not a "join" button, but an "Advisor
Partner" application, structurally identical to how Sandro Wealth's real site
already separates Advisors from Business Owners in its own navigation. The gate:
an advisor doesn't get community access by applying; they earn it by referring
qualified owners (a minimum number of vetted introductions per year, reviewed
annually), the same spirit as the provider scorecard.

On the mockup, this only needs to be a single "For Advisors" page with a short
qualification statement and an apply link. It doesn't need its own flow yet.

4. CAPABILITIES: WHAT CAN A BUSINESS OWNER/ADVISOR DO ON THE WEBSITE?

A diagnostic / assessment survey. It's the single highest-leverage thing on the
site, because it turns an anonymous visitor into a qualified, scored lead before
a human ever talks to them.

For the mockup, build one: a short Exit-Readiness / Value Gap Assessment (10 to
15 questions covering EBITDA range, growth stage, owner-dependency,
time-to-exit) that outputs a simple score and 2 to 3 flagged risk areas. This
doubles as the "onboarding audit" the Year-1 model already depends on. Don't try
to fake a real valuation calculator or trust-document review in the mockup; those
need real data models (Quist, Luminary) behind them and would look hollow as a
static demo.

The Sandro "5-step process" is an already-formalized internal methodology. That's
a prerequisite for this page.

5. CONTENT DEPTH: WHAT WILL THE AUDIENCE SEE/READ/ENGAGE WITH?

Don't aim for a Kitces-style publishing engine at launch. That's effectively a
full-time editorial job and the wrong scope for a pre-launch mockup. Recommend
three content types, small and high-signal: (NOTE: For mock-up this will be empty
or have existing few pieces of content from our website)

  - 8 to 12 flagship guides, mapped to the four inflection points (value
    acceleration, capital/growth, M&A, estate/wealth), written in a
    point-of-view voice, not generic advice content
  - 2 to 3 anonymized case studies, a redacted valuation snapshot and a redacted
    estate structure, doubling as proof of the provider "sample deliverable"
    mandate
  - Sandro Value proposition
  - A short founder Q&A, one or two pieces in an Alt Goes Mainstream interview
    voice, not a weekly cadence, since there's no host or producer function
    built yet
    [NOTE, not in the client doc: Travis has ruled out founder-interview framing
    in Sandro client materials. This bullet is deliberately not built.]

6. REFERENCE ELEMENTS: FROM OTHER LEADING COMMUNITIES

  - From Kitces.com: the "Start Here" onboarding path for first-time visitors,
    and the map/directory format (their AdvisorTech Map) applied to the provider
    directory, categorized, filterable, credibility-forward
  - From Global Family Office Community: the Expert Councils structure (peer
    groups organized by topic, not one undifferentiated forum) as a FUTURE model
    for member cohorts by inflection point; the Approved Supplier Directory
    pattern for the provider roster; and, most importantly, the gate mechanic,
    "contact us to apply for membership," with no self-serve signup button
    anywhere on the site
  - From Alt Goes Mainstream: just the interview and editorial voice for future
    content, not the distribution model. AGM is deliberately open because its
    business is audience size; this platform is deliberately closed because its
    business is trust. Borrow the format, not the openness

7. MEMBERSHIP MECHANICS AND JOURNEY: HOW SOMEONE ACTUALLY JOINS

Structure: Free membership. The interview and application gate, already scoped in
the website mockup brief, is the exclusivity mechanic, not price. The assessment
tool doubles as the qualifying step: an owner completes it, gets a score and
flagged gaps, and that output feeds directly into whether and how they're invited
to apply.

Confirmation flow: Apply, then assessment, then a short review (internal, not
automated), then either an invitation to a scoping conversation with a Sandro
partner, or a polite decline with a resource (the flagship guide library) offered
instead. No one is auto-approved into "membership" with no human touch. The human
step is the product.
