Page band with the brand's vertical rhythm.

```jsx
<Section tone="dark" size="md"> … </Section>
```

`tone` `dark|deep|wash|live` flip the whole semantic layer to `.sb-dark` automatically. `live` is the travelling signature gradient — one per page at most. One dark field or one light field per screen as the base — do not alternate more than a couple of times. `width="narrow"` for prose.

`Section` has no texture prop. A ray-echo field texture existed and was
removed — it read as a graphic pasted over the field rather than as light. If a
light band needs relief, change its `tone` or give it a photograph.
