The Sandro lockup — use wherever the brand signs a surface (header, footer, cover, letterhead).

```jsx
<Logo unit="business" tone="ivory" height={32} href="/" />
```

`unit="wealth"` sets the parent brand's wordmark. `symbolOnly` for favicons and avatars. Give it clear space of at least `height` on every side, keep `height >= 28` (the rays vanish below that), and align it to the same left edge as the rest of the composition.
