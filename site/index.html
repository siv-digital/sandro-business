How membership works

Anyone can apply. It starts with the assessment. Your assessment score is yours to keep.

Four steps, from assessment to admission.

1

Take your assessment

Assessment

15 questions on earnings, growth stage, how your business runs without you, and timing. You get a score, a starting track, and what next steps you should take.

2

Twenty minutes with a Sandro consultant

Consultation

A free call to go through your score, hear how the business runs, and talk through what a baseline valuation would show.

3

A partner reviews your results

Review

Nobody is admitted automatically. This review looks at scale, fit, and how our network can help you.

4

You hear back either way

Answer

An invitation to join, or helpful information you can take with you. Both come from the person who read your results.

How we build our partner ecosystem

No provider pays to be here.

In most owner networks the firms inside are paying for their place, which makes the referral list a roster of advertisers. Sandro takes no money from any provider. A place here cannot be bought, so a firm reaches you because it fits the problem in front of you and for no other reason. Every firm we introduce agrees to the same terms, in writing.
