For advisors

Sandro Business is built for owners. Advisors reach it through the owners they bring.

Access is earned by referral, not by application.

There is no advisor membership to buy and no join button to press. Advisors who refer qualified owners are given access to the community, the partner directory and the concierge desk on the same terms as members. That access is reviewed once a year against the referrals made.

Who qualifies

You advise business owners preparing to grow or sell, in a fiduciary or professional capacity.
The owners you work with sit in the range Sandro Business is built for.
You are willing to make introductions where the fit is real, and to say so when it is not.
Start the advisor conversation

No compensation moves in either direction. Sandro takes nothing from the firms in the directory, and pays nothing for a referral.
